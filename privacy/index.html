The Fertigation Binder

Privacy policy

How this fertigation binder app handles information. We do not sell grower data. The product is a progressive web app hosted on Netlify.

Who operates this product

The Fertigation Binder is logging software operated as a platform. Owners subscribe per facility. Floor techs do not create accounts. Privacy questions go through the owner or operator sign-in in the app — not a public inbox on this page.

What we collect

  • Fertigation logs — collection date, zone, cultivar, feed and runoff mL / pH / EC, tech initials, and notes entered on the floor tablet.
  • Facility and room config — facility name and location, rooms, flower cycles, floor PIN hash, and target bands an owner sets.
  • Owner and operator email — used only to send a magic-link sign-in. Floor unlock uses a 4-digit PIN, not an email account.
  • Optional integration keys — if an owner pastes a third-party key in facility settings, it stays in that browser’s local storage. v1 does not send it out.
  • Connection settings — the project URL and anon key pasted on first run stay in local storage on that device.

Where it lives

Binder data lives in the empty project the owner connects. Magic-link delivery uses that project’s email auth. The PWA is served from Netlify (static files and ordinary CDN request logs). The floor PIN is stored as a SHA-256 hash, not in plaintext.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell grower data, fertigation logs, or facility lists.
  • We do not use grower logs for advertising or third-party marketing.
  • This product is the Netlify-hosted PWA. There is no App Store binary.

Who can see a facility

Owners see the facilities they create. Floor techs with the PIN can log on an active facility. The platform operator can review pending facilities for approval and pause or remove a grow. The operator does not impersonate the owner on the floor path.

Retention and requests

Logs stay in the connected project until an owner or the operator removes the facility. Ask for a copy or deletion from the signed-in owner or operator account in the app.
