The Fertigation Binder

Privacy policy

How this fertigation binder app handles information. We do not sell grower data. The product is a progressive web app hosted on Netlify.

Who operates this product

The Fertigation Binder is logging software operated as a platform. Owners subscribe per facility on this product. Floor techs do not create accounts.

Ask a question

No sign-in required. This lands on the operator queue.

What we collect

  • Fertigation logs — collection date, zone, cultivar, feed and runoff mL / pH / EC, tech initials, and notes entered on the floor tablet.
  • Facility and room config — facility name and location, rooms, flower cycles, floor PIN hash, and target bands an owner sets.
  • Owner and operator email — used only to send a magic-link sign-in. Floor unlock uses a 4-digit PIN, not an email account.
  • Optional integration keys — if an owner pastes a third-party key in facility settings, it stays in that browser’s local storage. v1 does not send it out.
  • Public asks — name, facility, email, and message if you use Ask a question or Request a House quote on these pages.

Where it lives

Fertigation logs, facility config, and public asks live in the product database. Owner sign-in is an email magic link. Floor unlock is a 4-digit PIN on an approved facility. The PWA is served from Netlify (static files and ordinary CDN request logs). The floor PIN is stored as a SHA-256 hash, not in plaintext.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell grower data, fertigation logs, or facility lists.
  • We do not use grower logs for advertising or third-party marketing.
  • This product is the Netlify-hosted PWA. There is no App Store binary.

Who can see a facility

Owners see the facilities they create. Floor techs with the PIN can log on an active facility. The platform operator can review pending facilities for approval and pause or remove a grow. The operator may open a facility’s owner dashboard for support (rooms, targets, logs, settings). That view is not the floor: no PIN, no Add entry, no tech initials. The operator does not impersonate a tech on the cart.

Retention and requests

Logs stay in the product database until an owner or the operator removes the facility. Use Ask a question above for a copy or deletion request — no sign-in required.
