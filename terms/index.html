The Fertigation Binder

Terms of use

These terms cover the Fertigation Binder progressive web app — logging software for feed and runoff by room. Owners subscribe per facility.

The product

The Fertigation Binder is a greenhouse-floor tablet PWA hosted on Netlify. Floor techs unlock a facility with a PIN and record collections. There is no App Store binary.

v1 includes owner onboarding, facility cards, floor PIN logging, flower cycles, and feed/runoff collections. Extra modules are not part of this release.

Accounts and access

  • Platform operator — signs in at /admin. Approves, pauses, or removes facilities. Does not run owner onboarding.
  • Owners — sign in with a magic-link email, create facilities, set rooms, target bands, and the floor PIN.
  • Floor — no account. Unlock works only while the facility is active. Keep the PIN on the grow.

You are responsible for who you give the PIN to and for the accuracy of logs entered under that PIN.

Subscription

Billing is per facility, monthly, after a 14-day owner trial. The v1 list price is on Pricing ($49/facility/month). Checkout is not collected inside this PWA yet — starting a trial uses owner sign-up. The operator may change the published rate later. No seats.

Your data

Fertigation logs and facility/room config belong to the grow that created them. Optional integration keys stay in the browser. We do not sell grower data. Connect a new empty project for this product; do not point the app at another operation’s live database.

Acceptable use

Use the binder for your own facilities. Do not attempt to access another grow’s project or the operator dashboard without an allowlisted email.

No agronomic warranty

The binder records collections and compares them to bands you configure. It is not agronomic advice and does not guarantee crop outcome. Target chips are tools, not a prescription.
