Terms of use

These terms cover the Fertigation Binder progressive web app — logging software for feed and runoff by room. Owners run one binder per facility. The product is free through December 31, 2026.

The product

The Fertigation Binder is a greenhouse-floor tablet PWA hosted on Netlify. Floor techs unlock a facility with a PIN and record collections. There is no App Store binary.

Binder is what is live: owner onboarding, facility cards, floor PIN logging, flower cycles, and feed/runoff collections. Floor, Link, and House software modules are not shipping in the app today.

Accounts and access

  • Platform operator — signs in at /admin. Approves, pauses, or removes facilities. Does not run owner onboarding.
  • Owners — sign in with a magic-link email, create facilities, set rooms, target bands, and the floor PIN.
  • Floor — no account. Unlock works only while the facility is active. Keep the PIN on the grow.

You are responsible for who you give the PIN to and for the accuracy of logs entered under that PIN.

Subscription

Software is per facility, no seats. Coaching is a separate retainer. The live product is Binder. It is free through December 31, 2026 for every facility. After that date Binder is $49/facility/month on Pricing. When paid billing starts in 2027, new Binder facilities include a 14-day owner trial. Checkout is not collected inside this PWA — owner sign-up starts Binder.

Published coming rates (not for sale today): Floor $149/facility/month and Link $249/facility/month. House is a custom quote. The operator may change published rates later.

Your data

Acceptable use

Use the binder for your own facilities. Do not attempt to access another grow’s data or the operator dashboard without an allowlisted email.
